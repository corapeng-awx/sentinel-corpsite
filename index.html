The challenge

Fraud hits every stage of a payment. Most tools only watch one.

Card testing floods your checkout. Stolen credentials clear authorization. Bots strip inventory during flash sales. Friendly fraud surfaces weeks after delivery. Chargebacks stack up, and scheme penalties follow. Every stage has a different attack pattern, a different signal set, and a different cost if you miss it.

Sentinel connects them all into a single system that learns across the entire lifecycle.

Checkout & payment

Card testing

Automated validation of stolen cards through high-volume small transactions at checkout

Transaction fraud

Stolen credentials used for unauthorized purchases at authorization

Account takeover

Compromised credentials used to hijack accounts and make unauthorized purchases

Bot abuse

Automated scripts exploiting promotions, inventory, and payment flows during flash sales

Post-purchase

Delayed capture fraud

Authorization passes but fraud signals emerge before settlement

Friendly fraud

Legitimate purchases disputed as unauthorized after delivery

Refund abuse

Exploiting return policies through false claims or serial refund patterns

Disputes

Chargebacks

Scheme-mandated reversals requiring evidence and timely response

Scheme monitoring

Visa VAMP and Mastercard thresholds that carry financial penalties

Revenue loss

Disputed funds held or lost, compounded by scheme fees and penalties

How it works
Multi-stage AI decisioning in milliseconds

Every transaction flows through a real-time pipeline that scores, decides, and optimizes without slowing checkout.

1

Profile and model selection

Each transaction is mapped to specialized risk profiles based on business type and payment context. Digital goods, travel bookings, subscriptions, and high-risk categories each get tuned models, not a one-size-fits-all score.

2

Feature computation and risk scoring

Thousands of features computed per transaction in real time. The engine sees what static rules can't.

device fingerprint behavioral biometrics velocity analysis graph clustering IP geolocation impossible travel emulator detection issuer preferences
3

Layered rule evaluation

Risk scores flow through four rule layers: adaptive fraud typology rules that evolve with new attack patterns, segment-specific rules tuned per vertical, scheme compliance rules to maintain Visa and Mastercard standing, and merchant-specific rules for targeted strategies.

4

End-to-end payment optimization

Fraud decisions feed directly into Optimize 360 to orchestrate the complete payment journey. Smart 3DS authentication, intelligent acquiring path routing, and adaptive retry logic, all informed by fraud signals. Security applied only when risk justifies it.

Data foundation
Thousands of signals per transaction

The fraud engine evaluates a comprehensive risk data layer in real time. High-quality data directly improves model accuracy, reduces false declines, and helps maintain scheme compliance.

Device and biometrics

Device fingerprinting, emulator and VM detection, cookie manipulation, mouse movements, typing speed, and session consistency checks.

Behavioral patterns

Velocity analysis across multiple time windows, impossible travel detection, and abnormal sequence identification using deep learning models.

Network and identity

IP geolocation, VPN and proxy detection, email validation, phone verification, location inconsistency checks, and prepaid SIM or VOIP patterns.

Graph analysis

High-risk community detection, fraud density within connected entities, cluster velocity patterns, and abnormal network composition analysis.

Issuer intelligence

Issuer-specific approval patterns, authentication preferences, bank-level response characteristics, and historical performance metrics.

Payment context

Card BIN attributes, transaction amount and currency patterns, merchant category, name and address validation, and gibberish detection.
